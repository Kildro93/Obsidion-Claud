**Leitidee / Kurzbeschreibung**  
The lecture “FWFDEN11” is part of the module “Grundlagen für den Englischunterricht” and provides subject-specific foundations for teaching English at primary school.
 
The focus is on key concepts in linguistics and language acquisition research, as well as their relevance for learning and teaching foreign languages. Students develop an understanding of linguistic structures, multilingualism, and language policy in the school context.
 
Enrolment in the seminar “FWFDEN1” automatically includes participation in the lecture. The lecture does not appear in the enrolment portal (ESP).  
**Zu erreichende Kompetenzen**  
**Specific competence goals of the module:**   
Students:

- gain a basic understanding of linguistics and language acquisition research in the context of English language teaching.
- understand key concepts such as multilingualism, language development, language and cultural awareness.
- are able to use subject-specific knowledge to analyse learning and teaching sequences.
- recognise the relationship between linguistic insights and classroom practice.
 
**Lerninhalte**

- Foundations of linguistics in the context of English language teaching
- Multilingualism and linguistic diversity
- Language acquisition processes and constructivist learning approaches
- Lexico-grammatical structures and mental lexicon
- Learner language, language and cultural awareness
- Language change and language families
- Language policy frameworks and the CEFR

**Lehr- und Lernmethoden**  
Lecture with input sessions, working with academic texts and guided learning tasks to deepen and reflect on subject-specific content.
