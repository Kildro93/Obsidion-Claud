**Modul-Anlassnummer**  
0-26HS.P-B-PRIS-FWFDEN1.EN/AGf  
**Leitidee / Kurzbeschreibung**  
The module provides students with the initial foundations in the form of knowledge, skills and abilities to promote and support the foreign language learning process (English) of primary school children. Students also develop an understanding of the influence of external factors such as educational policy requirements or socio-cultural conditions on the teaching of English at primary school level in the Swiss-German context.  
**Zu erreichende Kompetenzen**  
The students:

- have basic knowledge of the acquisition of a foreign language at primary level, basic foreign language didactic concepts and current principles of foreign language teaching (competence-orientation, content- and action-orientation).
- are able to plan learning sequences that develop pupils' communicative language skills, based on the planning model used in the practical studies.
- can apply their basic didactic knowledge to the critical analysis of teaching materials.
- can understand foreign language teaching at primary level in its wider language policy context and reflect on their own attitude towards it.
- develop an awareness of multilingualism, languages and cultures as well as strategies that are effective across languages.
- further develop their job-specific language skills in English
 
The module contributes to the following general competence objectives of the PH FHNW :

- Participation in the profession-specific discourse (1)
- Organisation of development, learning and educational processes (2)
- Learning and development (3)
 
**Lerninhalte**  
In this introductory module, subject-specific didactic and academic content is developed, linked together and concretised in the planning of English lessons:
 
**Disciplinary knowledge (Fachwissenschaft)**

- Multilingualism and linguistic diversity
- Methods of foreign language teaching from the beginning of the 20th century to the present day
- Language learning processes and constructivist understanding of learning
- Lexico-grammar
- Learner language and learner language analysis
- Language change and language families
- Awareness of languages and cultures
- National and European language policy
- Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR)
 
**Subject didactics (Fachdidaktik)**

- Current principles of foreign language teaching at primary level: competence-orientation, content- and action-orientation
- Formulation of learning objectives
- Supporting the learning process in the four communicative competences of listening, reading, speaking and writing
- Use and significance of chunks (formulaic language patterns) in foreign language teaching
- Development of strategies
 
**Lehr- und Lernmethoden**  
The module consists of a seminar (subject didactics) and a lecture (disciplinary knowledge).  
Work is organized in thematic blocks and supported by milestone documents, which include the learning objectives of the blocks as well as exercises and transfer tasks. The milestone documents make the learning process visible and allow for self-assessment. The planning of a lesson sequence is accompanied.
